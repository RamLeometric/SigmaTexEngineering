About us

Engineering safe, dependable lifts since 2017

A Pune engineering company building safe, dependable lifts and material-handling systems since 2017.

Who we are

9+ years of hands-on engineering

Sigma Tex Engineering was established on 1 July 2017 with a simple aim: to give customers lifts and material-handling equipment that are engineered properly, installed carefully and looked after for the long term.

Our work spans passenger and building lifts, stainless steel and glass elevators, goods and hydraulic lifts, cranes and hoists, and industrial safety fencing. Because the company is led by a mechanical engineer, every job starts with the fundamentals — loads, clearances, structure and safety — before anything is fabricated or installed.

We serve residential societies, bungalows, commercial buildings, hospitals, factories, warehouses and cold storages across Pune and Maharashtra, and we stay with our customers after hand-over through maintenance contracts and breakdown support.

Meet the founder

Suresh Bapurao Kharat

Founder & CEO · B.E. (Mechanical)
“A lift or a crane is something people trust with their safety every day. Our job is to engineer it so that trust is never misplaced.”

A mechanical engineer by training, Mr. Kharat founded Sigma Tex Engineering in 2017 and personally leads its projects — from the first site survey and design through installation, testing and after-sales service. His engineering background shapes the company's focus on correct design, quality workmanship and safe operation.
