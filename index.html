About Sigma Tex

Engineering-first since 1 July 2017

Founded by Suresh Bapurao Kharat, B.E. (Mechanical), Sigma Tex Engineering brings sound mechanical engineering to every lift and material-handling job — however big or small.

We work directly with builders, architects, housing societies, factory owners and facility managers across Pune and Maharashtra. Every project is measured, designed and installed by our own team, and backed by responsive after-sales service.

  • Site survey & shaft design
  • Quality components
  • Safety-compliant installation
  • Transparent quotations
  • Load-tested before hand-over
  • Prompt after-sales support

How we work

From enquiry to hand-over

  1. Site survey

    We visit, measure the shaft or floor area and understand load, usage and budget.

  2. Design & quote

    You receive a clear drawing, specification and itemised quotation.

  3. Install & test

    Our team fabricates, installs and load-tests everything to safety norms.

  4. Hand-over & AMC

    Training for your staff, documentation, and ongoing maintenance support.
