Passenger & Building Lifts

Automatic and manual-door passenger lifts for residential, commercial and institutional buildings.

Passenger lifts designed around the building — from compact 4-person cars for bungalows and small apartments to 20-passenger lifts for offices, hospitals and commercial complexes. Hydraulic & Scissor Lifts

Hydraulic elevators, scissor lifts and platforms where a machine room or deep pit is not possible.

Hydraulic lifts use an oil power pack and cylinder in place of a traction machine, which makes them ideal for low-rise buildings, heritage structures, loading docks and heavy loads. Our scissor lifts and dock platforms help move goods and people between levels in workshops and warehouses.

  • Capacity from 500 kg to 5,000 kg
  • No machine room; minimal overhead required
  • Power pack, cylinder, guide rails and car frame supplied
  • Scissor lifts and dock levellers for industrial use
  • Ideal for
  • Low-rise buildings
  • Heritage structures
  • Loading docks
  • Workshops

FAQ

Questions customers often ask

Ask a question
How do I know which lift suits my building?

We start with a free site survey — measuring the shaft or available space, pit and headroom, and understanding how many people or how much load needs to move. We then recommend the lift type, capacity and door style, with a drawing and an itemised quotation.

Can you install a lift in an existing building?

Yes. Where there is no shaft, we can build a structural steel or glass shaft, and machine-room-less (MRL) or hydraulic lifts help where headroom or pit depth is limited. The survey tells us what is practical for your building.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on the number of floors, the lift type and whether civil work is ready. We share a clear schedule along with the quotation so you can plan around it.

Do you maintain lifts installed by other companies?

Yes. Our maintenance contracts cover lifts of all makes, with scheduled servicing, safety checks and breakdown attendance. We can also modernise older lifts with new controllers, drives and doors.

What safety features are included?

Our lifts include the safety devices required under the Maharashtra Lift Act — such as door interlocks, overload protection, over-speed governor and safety gear, emergency alarm and automatic rescue devices where specified.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Warje, Pune and serve customers across Pune and Maharashtra. Call or send an enquiry with your site location and we will confirm.

Planning a lift or a material-handling upgrade?

Share your site details — we'll visit, measure and send a clear quotation. The site survey is free.
